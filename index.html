about us

At Shri Shyam Safa House kota founder deepak singh mehar

we bring tradition, elegance, and pride to your most special occasions. With years of experience in royal safa tying and wedding turbans, we specialize in giving every groom and guest a majestic, cultural look that blends heritage with style. From classic Rajasthani safas to modern designer turbans, our team ensures perfect fitting, premium fabric, and elegant draping for every head we dress. Whether it's a wedding, cultural event, or celebration — we are here to make you look royal, proud, and graceful. We believe a well-tied safa is not just an accessory — it’s a symbol of honor.
